Privacy Policy

Cheese Heist: Mouse Escape

Effective date: October 29, 2026

The short version

Cheese Heist: Mouse Escape does not collect or transmit personal information. The game has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Information stored on your Mac

The game saves gameplay progress and preferences—such as unlocked levels, earned stars, sound, and language—in macOS local preferences (UserDefaults) on your device. This information is used only to provide and remember your game experience. The game does not send it to the developer or to third parties.

Third-party services

The game does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and it does not require an online account.

Your choices

You can clear saved game progress using the in-game clear-save option. Your macOS language and sound preferences can be changed in the game.

Changes to this policy

If the game’s data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date will change.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the project’s GitHub Issues page. Issues are public, so do not include private or sensitive information.

隐私政策

《奶酪大盗：鼠鼠逃脱》

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 29 日

简要说明

《奶酪大盗：鼠鼠逃脱》不会收集或传输个人信息。游戏不设账号、不含广告、分析或追踪功能。

保存在 Mac 上的信息

游戏会通过 macOS 本地偏好设置（UserDefaults）在你的设备上保存游戏进度和偏好，例如已解锁关卡、获得的星星、音效和语言设置。这些信息仅用于提供并记住游戏体验；游戏不会将其发送给开发者或第三方。

第三方服务

游戏不包含第三方分析、广告或追踪 SDK，也不需要在线账号。

你的选择

你可以使用游戏内的清档选项清除已保存的游戏进度，也可以在游戏中更改语言和音效偏好。

政策更新

如果游戏的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页面及生效日期。

联系开发者

如有隐私相关问题，请通过项目的 GitHub Issues 页面联系开发者。Issues 内容公开，请勿提交私人或敏感信息。
